Image Accessibility

Images are often helpful assets to a website. To make them useful for everyone, things to keep in mind include alternative text, length of descriptions on or around the image, color and contrast, as well as avoiding images that might cause illness. These are important for various users such as those who have turned off images in their browsers and those with low vision, color-blindness, or blindness.
